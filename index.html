How we work

Design. Build. Deliver.

Every stand goes through the same three stages. Watch each one at work.

Design

Our designers turn your brief into a stand concept, then show it to you in 3D from every angle before anything is built.

  • Concept design built around your brand story
  • Photo-real 3D visualisation of every area
  • Layouts for reception, lounge, meeting and display zones
  • Technical drawings ready for organiser approval

Build

We fabricate and install your stand with our own team, then supervise it on site so everything opens exactly as it was approved.
